About us

A web studio with its feet firmly in Mzansi.

MzansiCraft exists for one reason: too many good South African businesses are invisible online, or stuck with a website that nobody can load, find or trust. We fix that — properly, and at a price that makes sense in Rands.

The name

Why “MzansiCraft”?

Mzansi comes from the isiXhosa uMzantsi — “the south”. It is what South Africans call home, and it carries something a formal name cannot: familiarity, pride, and the sense that you are dealing with your own people.

Craft is the other half of the promise. Warmth is not enough on its own — the work has to be made with care: fast, secure, accessible, and built to hold up years after launch.

Put them together and you have the brief we give ourselves on every project: local at heart, serious about the craft.

What that means in practice

  • Prices quoted in Rands, invoiced in Rands
  • Built for phones on mobile data, not office fibre
  • WhatsApp treated as a first-class contact channel
  • .co.za domains, SA payment gateways, local delivery options
  • Support 24 hours a day, Monday to Saturday, by a person you know

What we stand on

Four things we will not compromise.

Plain language

No jargon used to sound impressive or hide a weak answer. If you cannot explain it simply, you do not understand it.

Ubuntu

“I am because we are.” Your business doing well is the whole point of the work — not a line item on an invoice.

Fair pricing

Fixed prices published openly on this site. No inflated quotes, no charges that appear only once you are committed.

No lock-in

Your domain, your content, your logins. If you ever want to move on, you take everything with you.

How we work

Small studio. Deliberately.

We take on a limited number of builds at a time. That is not a sales line — it is why replies come back the same day and why your project does not sit in a queue behind fifty others.

It also means we are honest about fit. If your project needs something we are not the right studio for, we will tell you that on the first call instead of taking the deposit.
