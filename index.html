Questions

The things people ask before they commit.

How much does a website cost in South Africa?

Our packages start at R2,500 for a single-page site, R5,500 for a 3–5 page business website and R9,500 for a 5–10 page site with a blog and SEO. Each one includes a year of domain registration and hosting, so there is nothing extra to buy in month one.

Online stores start from R14,500, priced on the size of your catalogue. Booking systems and custom features are quoted per project.

How long does it take to build?

A single-page site is usually live within a week. Most business websites launch in two to three weeks. The clock really starts once you have sent your content, logo and photos — that is the step that decides the pace.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting myself?

No. We register your .co.za (or .com) domain and set up hosting as part of the package, for the first 12 months. After that it is R650/year, or R250/month if you want maintenance included. The domain is registered in your name — it is yours.

Can I update the website myself afterwards?

Yes. We walk you through the parts you are likely to change, like prices, photos and opening hours. If you would rather not touch it, our maintenance plan covers content updates for you.

Do you work with businesses outside my province?

We work with businesses across all nine provinces — Gauteng, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, North West, Free State, KwaZulu-Natal, Eastern Cape, Northern Cape and the Western Cape. Everything runs over WhatsApp, email and video calls, so where you are makes no difference to the result.

How do I pay, and when?

A 50% deposit confirms your slot and starts the work. The balance is due before the site goes live. We accept EFT and bank deposit, and you get an invoice for your records.

Will the site load properly on mobile data?

That is a design requirement here, not an afterthought. We build mobile-first, compress every image and keep pages light so they open quickly on 3G and LTE — because that is how most of your customers will arrive.

What about POPIA and my customers' information?

Forms collect only what you actually need, everything is served over HTTPS, and your site ships with a privacy notice explaining what is collected and why — the groundwork the Protection of Personal Information Act expects. See our privacy policy for how MzansiTech itself handles data.
